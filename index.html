✦ The Rule of Light

The attic is pitched in total darkness. Your lantern reveals the path and banishes lurking shadows. Continuous beam emission builds heat—rest at checkpoints (E) to cool down, replenish health, and mend destroyed lanterns.

✦ Passive Senses

Even when turned off, your lantern hums and flickers warmly when hostile shadow entities lurk nearby in the darkness.
